Why this conversation matters

There are hundreds of MBA and PGDM programmes to choose from, and most of them look the same on paper — similar brochures, similar promises. But in an AI-driven job market, the right choice depends on things most students never think to check: how current the curriculum is, how the teaching method actually works, and whether the school is preparing you for the jobs that exist now, not five years ago.

This webinar breaks down exactly what to look for, from someone who has spent 25+ years inside management education , not from rankings or marketing claims.
